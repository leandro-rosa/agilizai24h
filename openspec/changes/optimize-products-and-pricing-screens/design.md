## Decisions

1. **One registry, no sync.** products-service stays the only owner of product, EAN, cost and price. Pricing reads it through the existing bulk reads; there is nothing to "synchronise". The "Sincronizar com a precificação" button was not a data refresh: it imported the pricing spreadsheet (new products, costs, prices). That import is kept, now under Import Excel, so no function is lost.
2. **Units.** `units_per_package` is already the box-to-unit factor and `package_type` the kind of box; `sale_unit` already exists. Added: `brand` and `purchase_unit` (the unit the supplier sells in: "CX", "FD", "UN"). The factor is one number on the product; an invoice line may carry its own (kept in the purchase original), and the cost is always stored per sold unit.
3. **Last cost vs average cost** stay two different numbers (Costs tab, from the previous change); the table shows only the last unit cost and its date, never an average.
4. **CMV** is already defined (finance-service: units sold × cost in force on the last day of the month, materialised). Nothing here changes it. A retroactive cost version in a closed month is flagged (`closed_month`) and the screens say which month needs the finance to be recomputed; nothing recomputes by itself.
5. **Import** is two calls: preview (pure, writes nothing) and apply (the same service the manual form uses, one product at a time, idempotent on SKU). Rules: SKU is the key; an EAN that belongs to another product is a conflict, never moved; an empty cell never erases an existing value unless the operator ticks "limpar campos vazios" for that run; no product is deleted.
6. **Pricing report freshness.** The stored run is not recomputed on every cost change. The report carries the time it was computed and the screen asks products-service for the latest change of cost or product; when newer, it says "há custos ou produtos novos desde o cálculo" with Recalcular. The approved price is untouched until someone approves.
7. **Historical vs current.** A period report values cost as of the last day of the period (the CMV rule). When the registry has a newer cost than that, the product shows "custo novo depois do período" with the new value and date; it is never shown as the period's cost.
8. **Coverage.** Analysed = products with a recommendation or a status other than insufficient data; the cards describe only those. Products without enough data are counted apart with the reason, never as margin zero.
9. **Impact** keeps the label "estimativa" and states its premise (volume held constant); it never promises that volume is kept.
10. **Publication.** No text says a price reached the POS or the card machine: there is no such integration confirming it.
