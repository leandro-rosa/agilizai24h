## Known gaps carried forward

- **Not exercised against the real Google Drive.** The Google client is behind `DriveClient` and was tested only through the in-memory fake; whether `Relatório_2026` is a file or a folder, the real names, and the Workspace policy are unconfirmed (1.2, 1.3, 13.3).
- **Thresholds are provisional.** 0.90 period identity and pooled coverage, 0.70 per store, 0.50 normal weekday and 3 edge days are judgment defaults, all configurable; the first real months calibrate them (13.4).
- **Expected operating days are inferred from the file**, not from an operating calendar (`stores-service` has none yet); a file cut short is caught by the edge check, a public holiday with no sales counts as an expected day, and a store that opens mid-month shows low coverage until a person validates it once.
- **Google Sheet hashes are best-effort** (an export's bytes can differ with unchanged content), and an earlier manual upload stores no hash, so an identical manual upload is not seen as a duplicate; the replace warning still applies.
- **Only network sales and restocking** come from the Drive; the old per-store sales format and costs stay manual.
- **The admin section was verified against a synthetic mock gateway**, not the real worker; the duplicate-reason wording was changed after that run and is covered by typecheck and lint only.
- **The gateway's shared upstream filter still drops the worker's `code`**; `DriveFilesController` relays the body itself. Extending the shared filter would serve other routes and is left as a follow-up.
